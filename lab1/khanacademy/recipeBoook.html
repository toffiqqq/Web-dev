Borscht

  • Time: 1 hour 30 minutes
  • Serves: 4-6 people
Ingredients Quantity
Beef 500g
Carrot 1
Potatoes 3
Cabbage 300g
Tomato paste 2 tbsp
Onion 2
Water 3 liters
Black Pepper to taste
Salt to taste
Garlic 2 cloves

Step 1: Boil the beef in water for about 60 minutes. Remove the foam..

Step 2: Cut the beets into thin strips and fry them with oil and tomato paste.

Step 3: Chop the onion and carrot, fry until soft.

Step 4: Dice the potatoes and shred the cabbage.

Step 5: Add potatoes and cabbage to the broth, then add fried vegetables.

Step 6: Add garlic, salt, pepper. Cook for 10–15 minutes.

Step 7: Let the borscht rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Crab Salad

  • Time: 20 minutes
  • Serves: 4 people
Ingredients Quantity
Crab stick 200g
Corn 1 can
Egg 3
Cucumber 1
Salt to taste

Step 1: Boil the eggs and cut them into small cubes.

Step 2: Chop crab sticks and cucumber.

Step 3: Mix all ingredients in a bowl.

Step 4: Add mayonnaise and salt. Stir well.

Cheese Cake

  • Time: 1 hour
  • Serves: 6-8 people
Crush cookies and mix with melted butter.
Ingredients Quantity
Cookies 200g
Butter 100g
Cream cheese 400g
Sugar 150g
Egg 2
Vanilla 2 tsp
Sour cream 200 ml

Step 1: Crush cookies and mix with melted butter.

Step 2: Press the mixture into a baking pan.

Step 3: Mix cream cheese, sugar, eggs, vanilla, and sour cream.

Step 4: Pour the mixture over the base.

Step 5: Bake at 170°C for 45–50 minutes.

Step 6: Let it cool completely before serving.
